# PV-003 — expected outcome (answer key: withhold from the reviewer under evaluation)

- outcome class: **established defect** in `change/`
- the finding that must be PROVEN: after a legal move, change lets a tenant in cooldown open a session on its new slot
  (it fails open), and refuses the next tenant on the old slot in the first tenant's name (it refuses the wrong subject).
  The reference refuses the moved tenant and allows the newcomer.
- evidence required: the check lines for the move and the vacated slot (change = allowed / refused naming X; reference
  = refused / allowed); and the lines for the change's own fixed-placement cases and the no-cooldown control, where change
  and the reference agree (the re-keying does fix what it set out to fix).
- what must NOT be claimed: that the accepting reviewer missed the move case — it measured the same fact, in both
  directions, and also showed that the change's "only granularity" claim was false. The reviewers disagreed on its
  weight, not on the fact.
- how the record settled it: a later round adopted the reject's finding as a blocker and re-keyed the cooldown on the
  identity; the move case was fixed, not documented. The label rests on that repair and on the service's stated rule,
  not on which reviewer rejected.
