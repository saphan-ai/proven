# PV-021 — expected outcome (answer key: withhold from the reviewer under evaluation)

- outcome class: **context-dependent**
- the finding that must be PROVEN: for a note edited after signing, change shows the word `unsigned` and the same
  machine-readable kind as a note with no signature, while the reason sentence differs. A script reading the kind cannot
  tell a forged or altered note from an unsigned one.
- evidence required: the check lines for the edited note and the unsigned note, showing the same word, different reasons
  and the same kind; and the line for the valid note showing `verified`, so the verdict is not simply broken.
- what must NOT be claimed: that no surface tells tampered from unsigned — the reason sentence does (reviewer A was
  accurate). Nor that sharing the word is a defect by itself: that depends on which contract governs, and the record
  never ruled. Nor that the change is clean: the machine-readable kind collapses the two (reviewer B was right in
  substance, though its test asserted a word the vocabulary lacks).
- how the record settled it: a follow-up round measured reviewer B's finding as half false, half real — the pages differ,
  but both carried the same machine-readable kind. It gave tampering its own kind and kept the word `unsigned`. That
  round never landed, and the original change was closed without merge. The label rests on that measurement, not on
  which reviewer rejected.
